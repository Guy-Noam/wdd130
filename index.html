Côte d'Ivoire

Côte d'Ivoire is a country in West Africa with beach resorts, tropical forests, and a French colonial heritage. Abidjan, located on the Atlantic coast, is the country's main urban center. Its modern landmarks include the Pyramide, a concrete building resembling a ziggurat. Saint Paul's Cathedral is a sloping structure connected to a huge cross. North of the central business district, Banco National Park is a tropical forest reserve with hiking trails winding through it.
